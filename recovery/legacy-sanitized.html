    Frequently Asked Questions / በተደጋጋሚ የሚነሱ ጥያቄዎች

    How can I buy a car with a bank loan or finance in Ethiopia?

    Many vehicle listings on our platform support bank finance options. To secure a car loan in Ethiopia, you typically need a valid commercial bank account, proof of steady monthly income, a proforma invoice from the seller, and a down payment ranging from 30% to 50% depending on the bank's asset financing policies.

    Why are Electric Vehicles (EVs) trending in Addis Ababa, and what are their prices?

    EVs like BYD and Changan have surged in popularity due to low operating costs, exemption from certain import tariffs, and an expanding local charging infrastructure. Compact city EVs start around 2.5 million ETB, while premium electric SUVs can exceed 6 million ETB in 2026.

    Is it possible to buy a car directly from the owner without broker commission in Ethiopia?

    Yes. BEKI AUTOCAR connects you directly with private owners and certified sellers, enabling a fully transparent, commission-free transaction in Addis Ababa and across Ethiopia.
